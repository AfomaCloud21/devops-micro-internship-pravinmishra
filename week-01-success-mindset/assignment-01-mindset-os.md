* etc.

### Answer Format

**Yes / No**

If Yes:

**What emotion did you feel?** (minimum 50–100 words)

## Answer

Yes, I have cut corners before. There was a time when I was overwhelmed and needed to submit an assignment urgently. Instead of doing the work myself, I collected a friend’s completed assignment, copied it, and submitted it as mine. 

At that time, I only did it to avoid punishment, but deep down, I knew I had not truly learned anything. After submitting it, I felt very unfulfilled and disappointed in myself. I realized that even though I had met the deadline, I had cheated myself out of growth and understanding.
Much later, I went back to redo the assignment on my own.  
Every time I take shortcuts like that, I feel empty because I know the success is not genuine.

# Reflection – Week 1

### Biggest insight I got about myself this week

I discovered that my mindset, not my circumstances, is what determines whether I grow. Even coming straight from holiday into an intense program, I found that when I truly want something, showing up becomes non-negotiable — I built a system instead of waiting to feel motivated.

### My biggest weakness/loop I noticed

I struggled to focus in the first few days, even though I genuinely love studying. The loop was staying in bed after the alarm rang, letting the comfort of rest pull me back instead of moving straight into study mode.

### One system I will implement from this week (exact habit + time)

Wake at 3:00 am and stand up immediately — no lying in bed after the alarm. Go straight to my study space and begin with a 50-minute focused block (Pomodoro: 50 min work, 10 min break), Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday.
